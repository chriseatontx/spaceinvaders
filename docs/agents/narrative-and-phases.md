# Narrative and Phase Rules

Read this file only when a task changes story content, glitches, phase transitions, the AI Interpreter handoff, breach, ARIA, or installment state.

Read the relevant section of `NarrativeandPlan.md` before editing.

## Canonical phases

- `manualCabinet`: the player directly controls the ship and may remain here indefinitely.
- `interpreter`: the AI Interpreter permanently controls movement and firing for that profile.
- `breach`: cabinet play stops during the transition.
- `mainframe`: the terminal-driven incremental game replaces cabinet control.

## Rules

- Only a central transition service may change canonical phase once that service exists.
- Do not trigger an irreversible transition from an ordinary upgrade purchase.
- Explain permanent consequences before confirmation.
- Installing the AI Interpreter removes manual control for that profile.
- Declining automation has no penalty and must not repeatedly interrupt play.
- Glitches must be brief, configurable, non-lethal, and must not cover hazards or steal input.
- Store accessible transcripts for missed or hard-to-read messages.
- Keep Lumen and ARIA identity/state in narrative data, not combat branches.
- The breach requires story qualification and explicit authorization. Score alone is insufficient.
- The first canonical breach includes at least 60 witnessed seconds of silence with only the CRT busy cursor.
- Do not advance hidden cabinet rewards during breach.

## Before completion

- Verify manual play is still indefinitely available in a manual profile.
- Verify every one-time trigger cannot repeat after reload.
- Verify irreversible transitions create the required checkpoint.
